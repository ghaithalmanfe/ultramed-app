  An original drawing made for this app (a glass molar: a see-through tinted body, a thin bright rim,

robot.svg
  An original drawing made for this app (a glossy white robot with a glass visor and glowing green eyes), no third-party
  material. It illustrates the "Sales advisor" card; the advice itself is worked out by the app from its
  own figures (no AI service is connected).
