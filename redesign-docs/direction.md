# The adopted direction (owner, 14 Nov) — read before designing anything

The owner settled the direction in a design conversation before he wrote the spec
(ULTRAMED_PREMIUM_REDESIGN.md is built on it). He confirmed on 14 Nov that this is
"the direction we adopted". Where the spec and this page differ, the SPEC wins
(colours and type come from the company's real site, never from memory).

## The look: "A + B + C"
A. Executive dashboard — formal, premium, a manager's command centre.
B. Modern minimal — light, clean, simple, fast: a LIGHT interface.
C. Mobile first — an excellent phone experience for the reps between clinics.
(Not D, a dense dark analytics wall.)

Identity table he adopted, and how it is applied here:
- Primary: UltraMed green → the site's deep green #022917 (brand extraction).
- Background: off-white. Cards: white. Borders: light grey hairlines.
- Corners: 12–16 px (12 controls and cards, 16 large panels) — softer than the site's 10.
- Sidebar: dark ("deep navy" in that conversation). Navy is not a brand colour and the spec says
  brand colours only → the sidebar is the brand's DEEP GREEN #022917 with the white/lime official
  logo and a lime active marker (as UltraMed's own app icon: lime on deep green).
- Typography: a clean modern sans → DM Sans (the site's own) + IBM Plex Sans Arabic; tabular figures.
- Icons: one thin line family (the app's own sprite, Lucide-like). Charts: minimal and clean.
- Lime #A5CF63 is the ONE accent (active states, one hero figure/card, chart emphasis) — never text on white.

## Home = a sales command centre (both roles; a rep sees only her own)
First row: monthly target achievement, sales (ERP files only), team performance, clinic coverage,
follow-ups due, prescriptions growth — each only where the app's own functions give it.
Then three blocks: Action centre (what needs attention today), Sales against target, Recent activity.

## Navigation: five groups (labels map to the app's REAL views and More entries — invent none)
1 Overview — Today (dashboard), KPI scorecard, Month by month.
2 Customers — Clinics, Doctors.
3 Sales — Products, Clinic sales analysis, prescriptions (the Doctors screen's prescriptions tab), orders where the app has an entry.
4 Field operations — Log visit, Calendar, Team (chat and memories), Requests / escalations / stands.
5 Intelligence — Report (with its coach and coverage), Selling playbook, Weekly management deck.
Supervisor-only entries (Admin, ERP import, backups, deck …) stay supervisor-only, in a quiet "Management" group.
Phone: bottom bar Today · Log visit · Clinics · Calendar · More (More holds everything else, same groups).

## His standing conditions
No function, field, figure or permission is lost. No fake numbers. The app must feel like a real
commercial product. He is short on usage budget: work economically.
